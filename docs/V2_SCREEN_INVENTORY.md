# CMS desktop screen inventory

The app exposes screens according to the signed-in identity's permissions. Collectors receive Dashboard, Customer Worklist, Collector Reports, and customer-specific Follow-up, Payment Posting, Follow-up History, Payment History, and Communications. Administrators receive user, role, master, risk, allocation, and audit screens. Owner reporting permissions expose performance and collector-performance views. Change Password and Appearance Settings are available from the application navigation.

Customer-specific workflows open from a selected Customer Worklist row. Use Back to Worklist to return to the customer list.
